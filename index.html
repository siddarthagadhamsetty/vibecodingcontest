Lineup

Two clocks, one student.

I’m Lahari, an engineering student at BITS Pilani, Hyderabad Campus. Economics and computer science sit on the same timetable: what people choose, what a price is saying, and how to write that down so a program can push it around.

Off the timetable I play badminton and football. A rally punishes a lazy foot. A match is a pile of decisions happening at once. I like both clocks — the long one in class, and the short one under lights.

A rally is a short market. Both clear fast, and both punish a slow read.
